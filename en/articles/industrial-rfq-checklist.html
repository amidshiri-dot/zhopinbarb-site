Preparing a manufacturing request for quotation

A structured checklist covering specifications, quantity, schedule and delivery destination for manufacturing inquiries.

Zhopin Barb | Published: 5 October 2026

Define the scope of the inquiry

A request for quotation describes the proposed order. A part name alone rarely establishes the complete scope. Identify the required component, application and quantity.

This article is a preparation guide. Feasibility, pricing and delivery dates are determined following review and agreement.

Identify technical documents

Provide available drawings, models or photographs with measurement units and revision identifiers. State the proposed material, critical dimensions, finish and additional processing. Mark unresolved specifications as review items.

Declare any required inspection reports or material documentation at the outset. Availability must be confirmed for the particular order.

Separate quantity and delivery requirements

Specify prototype and production quantities separately. Include the requested date and delivery location. International inquiries should identify the destination country and city, packaging requirements and proposed delivery arrangements.

Naming a destination does not confirm shipping availability. Feasibility and delivery arrangements require agreement before the order is finalized. Describe any staged deliveries separately.

Review the package before submission

Ensure filenames and document revisions match the inquiry. Include a contact person, item list and unresolved questions. Use consistent requirements when comparing supplier responses.

The Zhopin Barb form prepares inquiry text for copying or opening in an email application. Technical files can then be attached to the email. Preparing the text does not send or accept an order.
