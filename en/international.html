BUYER QUESTIONS

Information for prospective customers

Can I inquire about a single part?

Yes. Both one-off and batch requirements can be reviewed. The suitable production route depends on the part.

What if I do not have a complete drawing?

A photograph, sample or application brief can start the conversation. Required details are resolved before production.

How are delivery arrangements confirmed?

Order feasibility, packaging, shipping and delivery arrangements for your destination are reviewed and agreed before an order is finalized.

Are certificates or test reports available for every part?

Specify required documents in your inquiry. Availability of each certificate or report must be reviewed and confirmed for that order.
